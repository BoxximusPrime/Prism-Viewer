# Prism Viewer 0.6.1

This Windows update adds quick access to everyday settings, new photography
controls, and refined skin scattering and graphics defaults.

- **Quick Prefs** adds a toolbar floater with Default/Ultra shadow and PCSS/GTAO
  quality, draw distance, name tags, look-target visibility and hover height.
  Choose and cycle inventory/Library skies, water and day cycles, or return to
  the shared environment.
- **Photo composition guides** adds an Overlays tab with Rule of Thirds,
  Center Cross, Diagonals, Golden Ratio and mirrored Golden Triangle guides.
  Choose black or white lines and adjust opacity. Guides remain visible with
  normal UI hidden and stay out of saved photos.
- **Look at camera** in Photo Tools > Focus locally aims standard and Bento
  avatar eyes at the camera, including frozen poses. It leaves heads unchanged,
  sends no gaze updates, and restores normal eyes when disabled or Photo Tools closes.
- **Easier surface placement:** dragging the Move center square now snaps to
  rendered mesh or terrain automatically, without holding Ctrl+Shift.
- **Separate grazing blur** in Graphics > SSS smooths grazing scatter from
  sun/moon, point lights and projectors independently of transmission and
  ordinary diffusion, preserving skin texture and highlights.
- **Updated graphics defaults** incorporate the September 17 tuning, including
  skin scattering, bloom and water preferences. Geometric water displacement
  now defaults off. Scoped Default buttons use the updated values, and the
  existing startup prompt lets you adopt the changed graphics defaults or keep
  your saved preferences.

## Windows installation

Download the `Prism_0_6_1_*_x86_64_Setup.exe` asset. The installer is unsigned
and uses the existing separate Prism installation and profile directories.
Upgrading preserves saved preferences; the graphics reset is optional.
A SHA-256 checksum file is included.

Hardware and in-world appearance testing remains ongoing, particularly for
individual eye rigs, environment selection and skin-scattering tuning.
